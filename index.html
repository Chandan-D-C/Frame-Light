UNDERSTANDING THE FRAME.

Exposure

The amount of light reaching the sensor — governed by three settings working together.

  • Aperture
  • Shutter Speed
  • ISO

Aperture

Controls depth of field and how much light enters the lens per moment.

  • Wide = shallow blur
  • Narrow = more in focus
  • Affects light intake

Shutter Speed

How long the sensor is exposed — the difference between frozen and flowing motion.

  • Fast freezes motion
  • Slow blurs movement
  • Longer = more light

ISO

Sensor sensitivity to light — the trade-off between brightness and grain.

  • Low = clean, needs light
  • High = bright, more noise
  • Digital sensitivity control

Composition

How elements are arranged inside the frame to guide the eye.

  • Rule of thirds
  • Leading lines
  • Negative space

Light

The single most important variable in any photograph.

  • Golden & blue hour
  • Hard vs. soft light
  • Back & side light

Behind the Camera

I photograph the spaces between moments — the light that touches a building for only a few seconds, the reflection left behind after rain, the expression that disappears before anyone notices.

My work moves between architecture, street and portrait photography, always chasing the same thing: a frame that holds still long enough to be felt, not just seen.
